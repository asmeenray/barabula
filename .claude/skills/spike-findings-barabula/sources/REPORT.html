Five spikes

What the tests proved

001 · TriageValidated

The list is an index, not a source of truth

  • 453 entries screened in 10 relevant categories
  • 87% of doc links load; 10% are dead
  • Nothing in it says whether commercial use or storage is allowed, and that decided most verdicts
002 · WeatherValidated

Forecast or "typical" for every trip day

  • 7 cities × 3 trip shapes + edge cases: 220 calls, 0 errors, p50 35–43 ms
  • Open-Meteo and MET differ by 2.9 °C on Lisbon highs (0.7–1.3 °C elsewhere)
  • Meteostat vs ERA5, Lisbon: within 0.4–1.4 °C a month
003 · Trip cardValidated

"Know before you go" in under 0.5 s, no keys

  • 8 trips; card ready in 91–474 ms
  • Offline holidays found Eid in Morocco, Songkran, Golden Week
  • Time gap must use trip dates: London–Mumbai is 5.5 h in January, not today's 4.5 h

Out of date in the list

Traps to avoid

  • REST Countries: v3.1 is deprecated. v5 needs a key, and its free plan is "for prototyping, evaluation, and non-commercial use", with a 3-day cache limit. Ship country facts as static data instead.
  • caldays returns 2026 holidays whatever year you ask for, with no error.
  • Nager.Date has no India, Thailand or UAE, and "for commercial purposes we require active sponsorship".
  • Frankfurter has only about 30 currencies: no Moroccan dirham, Vietnamese dong or UAE dirham.
  • Open-Meteo is free only for non-commercial use, and long requests count as many calls. A 10-year request costs about 261 calls, which hit the per-minute limit within a few cities.
  • Pirate Weather now needs a key; APIXU, Teleport and WorldTimeAPI are gone; Eventbrite search shut in 2019.
  • Link-preview and search-scraping APIs (Microlink, Scrappa, Serply) fetch other sites' pages, which breaks the no-scraping rule.
The one plan that changes at phase 20

Weather: now, and after Barabula charges money

Now (free app)

Open-Meteo for the forecast (16 days) and for "typical" days further out. Ask for each past year separately (10 small calls, at most 3 at a time), not 10 years in one call. Cache forecasts for 1 hour and typical values for 30 days.

From phase 20

MET Norway for the forecast (free and commercial; about 9.5 days). "Typical" comes from normals already stored in our database (Meteostat or ERA5 fetched while still free). The other option is Open-Meteo's paid Professional plan, because its cheaper plan excludes historical and climate data.

nexu-io/open-design · reviewed read-only

Open Design: what it is, and why not now

An Electron desktop app, about 400 MB, that drives your coding agent (Claude Code, Codex and 24 others) to produce HTML mockups, decks, images and video from a brief and a design system. It's an open-source copy of Anthropic's Claude Design. It doesn't produce React. Its "Next.js export" is a prompt asking the agent to rebuild the HTML.

  • Nothing new where it matters. Barabula already has the same loop (GSD sketches → UI-SPEC → Impeccable → Claude Code), and Impeccable works on the real running app.
  • Privacy and safety cost. Its privacy policy says usage telemetry is on by default, and a "safety and reliability" channel is always on. Open bug #8560 turns telemetry back on after you opt out. It runs Claude Code with bypassPermissions, and open bug #4594 reports projects that aren't isolated.
  • Young and commercial. 38 releases in 5 months, 532 open issues, an upsell funnel, and rewards for starring the repo.
  • If you want a canvas tool, try Claude Design first. If you still want Open Design, run a one-hour sandbox: choose "Don't share", never point it at the Barabula repo, and don't install its MCP server.
Found on the way

Things this touches in the app and handover

  • Foursquare v3 is deprecated (15 May 2026). src/lib/places.ts still calls api.foursquare.com/v3/places/search from the chat route.
  • V5 answered: Foursquare's Pro endpoints are free for 0–500 calls a month since 1 June 2026, then $15 per 1,000. Tips, photos and premium fields such as hours and ratings start at $18.75 per 1,000, with no free calls.
  • Nominatim forbids autocomplete. The phase 16 "Where to?" picker must never call it per keystroke. GeoNames in Postgres fits instead.
  • ODbL: storing Nominatim coordinates one place at a time is fine. Copying OSM opening hours onto FSQ places can trigger share-alike, so fetch them on demand.
