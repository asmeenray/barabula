Walking times

Spike 004. One trip day's places in order. Each leg shows the no-API estimate (straight line × 1.3 at 4.8 km/h) against three public OSM routers. Places are found with Nominatim at 1 request a second, so the first load takes a few seconds.

Routing © OpenStreetMap contributors. Engines: OSRM demo (project-osrm.org), FOSSGIS OSRM foot and Valhalla (openstreetmap.de). Call log: /log
